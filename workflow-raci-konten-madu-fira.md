## Detail per Tahap

### 1. Brief
_Output: Dokumen brief konten · Estimasi: 2–3 jam · Tools: Notion, Google Docs, Miro_
- R: Social Media Lead, Social Media Strategist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Content Creator, Stakeholder
- I: Seluruh tim
- Catatan: Insight brief harus sampai utuh ke tim kreatif.

### 2. Riset
_Output: Temuan riset & catatan insight · Estimasi: 2–3 hari · Tools: Spreadsheet, TikTok Creative Center, Meta Library_
- R: Social Media Researcher, Data & Insight Analyst
- A: Social Media Strategist
- C: Social Media Specialist
- I: —
- Catatan: Ubah data jadi insight yang bisa ditindaklanjuti — jangan mengendap.

### 3. Strategi & Arah
_Output: Arah strategi + content pillar · Estimasi: 1–2 hari · Tools: Notion, Sheet_
- R: Social Media Strategist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Creative Director
- I: Tim eksekusi
- Catatan: Content pillar & objektif — kenapa, bukan cuma apa.

### 4. Ideation / Brainstorming
_Output: Daftar ide prioritas · Estimasi: 1–2 jam · Tools: FigJam, Google Meet, Notion_
- R: Creative Director, Content Creator, Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Graphic Designer, Video Editor
- I: Social Media Strategist
- Catatan: Ide besar diprioritaskan; simpan yang belum kepakai.

### 5. Planning / Content Calendar
_Output: Content Plan + Editorial Calendar · Estimasi: 1–2 hari · Tools: Notion, Trello, ClickUp, Sheet_
- R: Social Media Specialist, Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: Tim
- Catatan: Tanggal, topik, jenis, tujuan, PIC, platform, status.

### 6. Copywriting / Script
_Output: Draft caption/script final · Estimasi: 2–3 hari · Tools: Notion, Google Docs_
- R: Copywriter, Content Creator
- A: Creative Director
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: Graphic Designer
- Catatan: Copywriter & designer harus sinkron sejak awal.

### 7. Visual / Produksi
_Output: File konten siap upload · Estimasi: 2–4 hari · Tools: Canva, Figma, Adobe Suite, CapCut_
- R: Graphic Designer, Videografer / Video Specialist, Video Editor
- A: Creative Director
- C: Copywriter
- I: —
- Catatan: Hook 0–3 dtk, subtitle wajib, konsisten brand.

### 8. Review & Approval (Approval)
_Output: Konten disetujui · Estimasi: 1–2 hari · Tools: Notion, WhatsApp_
- R: Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead, Klien
- C: Creative Director
- I: Tim eksekusi
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Copywriting / Script
- Catatan: Kalau ditolak, balik ke Copywriting/Visual untuk revisi (maks 1–2×).

### 9. Upload
_Output: Log upload + screenshot · Estimasi: 15–30 mnt/konten · Tools: Meta Business Suite, TikTok Scheduler_
- R: Social Media Specialist, Content Creator
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: —
- I: —
- Catatan: Konten FINAL; revisi last minute = workflow belum sehat.

### 10. Engage / Community
_Output: Highlight komentar + respon · Estimasi: 1–2 jam/hari · Tools: IG App, TikTok App, Meta Inbox_
- R: Community Manager
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: —
- Catatan: Komentar audiens disaring & disampaikan ke tim strategi.

### 11. Analisis & Evaluasi (Loop)
_Output: Rekap performa + saran improvement · Estimasi: 1–2 hari/report · Tools: IG Insight, TikTok Analytics, Looker Studio_
- R: Data & Insight Analyst
- A: Social Media Strategist
- C: Social Media Lead
- I: Tim
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Brief
- Catatan: Learning nyambung ke perencanaan berikutnya — loop balik ke Brief.
