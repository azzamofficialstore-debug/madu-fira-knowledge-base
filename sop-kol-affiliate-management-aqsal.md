# SOP KOL & Affiliate Management: Aqsal

_Disusun via Nakama SOP Playbook — SOP Studio (bongkar pasang)_

_KOL dipelajari untuk dicari fit-nya (TRACE), bukan asal followers besar._

**Role Terlibat:** KOL Specialist, Strategist, Affiliator & Community Manager, Analyst

## 9 Langkah KOL Campaign

1. Campaign Brief & Objective (objective, target via KOL, budget, timeline, jumlah KOL+tier, platform, success metric)
2. KOL Selection & Evaluation (TRACE: Trust, Relevance, Authenticity, Conversion, Efficiency + cek red flags)
3. Outreach & Negotiation (benchmark rate card per tier + adjustment format)
4. Brief ke KOL (10 poin wajib; tanpa key message & tracking = belum layak dikirim)
5. Tracking Setup SEBELUM campaign (link unik, kode promo, UTM, baseline metrics)
6. Content Review (key message, do's & don'ts, tracking benar; revisi maks 1–2×)
7. Posting & Monitoring (sesuai jadwal, cek vs approved, capture insight komentar)
8. Performance Tracking H+7 dan H+30 (primary + secondary metrics per objective)
9. Evaluation & Report (overview, per KOL, top/bottom, ROI per tier, learnings, recommendation)

## Affiliate Program (4 Fase)

1. Akuisisi (rekrut yang fit, bukan asal banyak)
2. Aktivasi (onboarding, brief, target first sale 2 minggu)
3. Retensi (clustering: Pemula/Aktif/Performer/Leader + insentif per cluster)
4. Advokasi (top performer jadi ambassador, akses eksklusif)

## Checklist Final

- [ ] KOL lolos TRACE & bebas red flag (fake followers, engagement palsu)
- [ ] Brief 10 poin lengkap (key message + tracking ada)
- [ ] Tracking link/kode/UTM unik per KOL terpasang
- [ ] Konten sesuai do's & don'ts + brand identity hadir
- [ ] Distribusi GMV affiliate merata (waspada 80% dari 5%)

## Do's & Don'ts

**Do's**
- ✅ Evaluasi KOL pakai TRACE sebelum deal
- ✅ Setup tracking sebelum campaign jalan
- ✅ Brief tepat waktu dengan key message
- ✅ Cluster affiliate & beri insentif bertingkat

**Don'ts**
- ❌ Pilih KOL hanya dari followers besar
- ❌ Kirim brief tanpa key message/tracking
- ❌ Abaikan red flag fake engagement
- ❌ Biarkan GMV terkonsentrasi di sedikit affiliate
