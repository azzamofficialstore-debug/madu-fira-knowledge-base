# KPI & Review Framework: Madu Fira

5 role · mode target: Aggressive (+40%+).

Prinsip: Individual KPI → Social Media KPI → Marketing KPI → Business KPI. Kalau koneksi putus, ada yang salah di strategi.

## Hierarki KPI 4 Level

| Level | Owner | Contoh KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 — Business KPI | Owner / C-Level | Revenue/leads dari sosmed, CAC via sosmed, market share growth, brand equity |
| Level 2 — Marketing KPI | Marketing Manager | Brand awareness, website traffic dari sosmed, conversion rate, lead generation, brand sentiment |
| Level 3 — Social Media KPI | Social Media Lead / Tim | Reach, engagement rate, follower growth, content performance, share of voice, save rate, response rate & time |
| Level 4 — Individual KPI | Per Role | Detail per role (proses / output / outcome) |

## KPI per Role (Proses / Output / Outcome)

### Social Media Strategist
- **Proses:** Melakukan riset audiens, tren, & kompetitor secara rutin.; Menyusun strategi & brief sebelum tiap campaign.; Memimpin evaluasi performa bulanan.
- **Output:** Jumlah strategi atau campaign plan yang tersusun.; Kualitas insight riset yang digunakan tim; Dokumen evaluasi & rekomendasi bulanan.
- **Outcome:** Peningkatan konsistensi performa konten antar periode.; Kesesuaian arah konten dengan objektif brand.; Peningkatan efektivitas tim dalam menjalankan strategi.

## Cara Menentukan Target

1. **Baseline:** Data 3 bulan terakhir, hitung rata-rata
2. **Benchmark:** Bandingkan dengan industri, kompetitor, platform average
3. **Target:** Conservative (+10–15%), Moderate (+20–30%), Aggressive (+40%+)
4. **Breakdown:** Annual → quarterly → monthly; team → individual

_Aturan: target bisa di-influence oleh yang diukur, achievable tapi stretching, di-review tiap quarter._

## Measurement Cadence

| Frekuensi | Yang Diukur | Tindakan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harian | Status posting, engagement real-time, community | Quick flag anomali |
| Mingguan | Snapshot performa, output vs target | Weekly check-in 15–30 menit |
| Dua Mingguan | Trend analysis, pattern | Mini review |
| Bulanan | Full metrics, KPI vs target | Monthly review 60–90 menit |
| Quarterly | KPI achievement, strategy review | Strategic review 2–3 jam |

## Konsekuensi KPI

- Tercapai: recognition, learning sharing, reward, naikkan tanggung jawab
- Gap ≤10%: coaching conversation
- Gap 10–30%: improvement plan 1 bulan
- Gap >30%: deep assessment (skill / resource / role fit?)
- Berulang 3 bulan: Performance Improvement Plan formal

_Selalu lihat KONTEKS: target tak tercapai karena resource kurang adalah masalah sistem, bukan individu._
