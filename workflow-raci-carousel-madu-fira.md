## Detail per Tahap

### 1. Riset Topik & Angle
_Output: Topik + poin nilai · Estimasi: 1–2 jam · Tools: Sheet, AnswerThePublic_
- R: Social Media Researcher, Content Creator
- A: Social Media Strategist
- C: —
- I: —
- Catatan: Cari pertanyaan/pain point yang layak dibahas mendalam.

### 2. Outline 8–10 Slide
_Output: Struktur slide · Estimasi: 1–2 jam · Tools: Google Docs_
- R: Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Content Creator
- I: —
- Catatan: Slide 1 hook, slide isi bertahap, slide akhir CTA.

### 3. Copywriting per Slide
_Output: Teks final tiap slide · Estimasi: 2–3 jam · Tools: Google Docs_
- R: Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: Graphic Designer
- Catatan: Satu ide per slide; teks ringkas, mudah dipindai.

### 4. Desain Carousel
_Output: File carousel siap · Estimasi: 3–5 jam · Tools: Figma, Canva_
- R: Graphic Designer
- A: Creative Director
- C: Copywriter
- I: —
- Catatan: Hirarki visual jelas, konsisten brand, mudah dibaca di HP.

### 5. Review & Approval (Approval)
_Output: Carousel disetujui · Estimasi: 30–60 menit · Tools: Notion_
- R: Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Creative Director
- I: —
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Desain Carousel
- Catatan: Ditolak → balik ke Desain.

### 6. Publish + Caption
_Output: Tayang · Estimasi: 15–30 menit · Tools: Meta Business Suite_
- R: Social Media Specialist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: —
- I: —
- Catatan: Caption + first comment (hook & CTA).
