# SOP Ads / Performance Marketing: Faiz

_Disusun via Nakama SOP Playbook — SOP Studio (bongkar pasang)_

_Mulai dari objective jelas; testing & tracking sebelum scale._

**Role Terlibat:** Strategist, Ads Specialist, Copywriter, Designer/Video Editor

## 7 Langkah Ads Management

1. Campaign Strategy & Brief (objective, audience, budget total+harian, timeline, platform, success metric)
2. Creative Brief ke tim konten (key message, format+spec, 2–3 variasi A/B, CTA, tone)
3. Creative Production (copy + visual/video, min. 2–3 variasi, review internal)
4. Campaign Setup (objective/audience/budget/bidding, upload creative, tracking pixel+UTM+conversion event)
5. Testing & Monitoring (A/B test 1 variabel, biarkan 3–5 hari, monitor spending/CTR/CPC/CPA harian)
6. Optimization (matikan underperform, scale perform bertahap maks 20–30%, refresh creative bila fatigue)
7. Reporting & Evaluation (overview, key metrics vs target, creative performance, audience insights, learnings, next steps)

## Checklist Final

- [ ] Hook visual/teks kuat di 3 detik pertama
- [ ] Key message tersampaikan tanpa baca semua
- [ ] CTA jelas & tidak melanggar kebijakan platform
- [ ] Format teknis sesuai
- [ ] Tracking (pixel/UTM/conversion event) terpasang sebelum launch

## Do's & Don'ts

**Do's**
- ✅ Mulai dari objective jelas
- ✅ Testing min. 2–3 variasi
- ✅ Monitor harian + dokumentasikan learning
- ✅ Pastikan tracking sebelum launch
- ✅ Koordinasi dengan tim organik

**Don'ts**
- ❌ Launch tanpa brief tertulis
- ❌ Boost post tanpa strategi
- ❌ Judge terlalu cepat (<3 hari)
- ❌ Naikkan budget drastis
- ❌ Hanya lihat CTR tanpa conversion
