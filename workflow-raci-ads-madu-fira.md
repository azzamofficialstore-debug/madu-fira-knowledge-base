# Workflow & RACI: Ads Madu Fira

_8 tahap · disusun via Nakama SOP Playbook — Workflow & RACI Studio_

## Alur Tahapan

1. **Campaign Strategy & Brief** →
2. **Creative Brief ke Tim** →
3. **Creative Production (variasi A/B)** →
4. **Campaign Setup** →
5. **Testing & Monitoring** →
6. **Hasil A/B Test?** [Percabangan] →
   ◇ Ada variasi yang menang?
   ├─ Ya — scale → Scale & Optimization
   ├─ Belum — iterasi → Creative Production (variasi A/B)
7. **Scale & Optimization** →
8. **Reporting & Evaluation** [Loop]
   ↩ balik ke: Campaign Strategy & Brief

## Detail per Tahap

### 1. Campaign Strategy & Brief
_Output: Brief + target KPI · Estimasi: 2–4 jam · Tools: Sheet, Ads Manager_
- R: Performance Marketer, Social Media Strategist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: —
- I: Stakeholder
- Catatan: Objective, target, budget, KPI (CPA/ROAS).

### 2. Creative Brief ke Tim
_Output: Creative brief · Estimasi: 2–4 jam · Tools: Notion_
- R: Social Media Strategist
- A: Creative Director
- C: Performance Marketer
- I: —
- Catatan: Terjemahkan angle jadi arahan kreatif + variasi hook.

### 3. Creative Production (variasi A/B)
_Output: Set variasi kreatif · Estimasi: 3–7 hari · Tools: Figma, CapCut_
- R: Creative Director, Graphic Designer, Video Editor, Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Performance Marketer
- I: —
- Catatan: Buat 3–5 variasi hook/format untuk diuji.

### 4. Campaign Setup
_Output: Campaign siap live · Estimasi: 0,5–1 hari · Tools: Ads Manager_
- R: Performance Marketer
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Data & Insight Analyst
- I: —
- Catatan: Struktur campaign, audience, pixel & tracking benar.

### 5. Testing & Monitoring
_Output: Data awal per variasi · Estimasi: 3–7 hari · Tools: Ads Manager, Analytics_
- R: Performance Marketer, Data & Insight Analyst
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: —
- I: —
- Catatan: Uji variasi; jangan ubah terlalu cepat sebelum data cukup.

### 6. Hasil A/B Test? (Percabangan)
- R: Performance Marketer
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Data & Insight Analyst
- I: —
- ◇ Kondisi: Ada variasi yang menang?
- ├─ Kalau **Ya — scale** → lanjut ke: Scale & Optimization
- ├─ Kalau **Belum — iterasi** → lanjut ke: Creative Production (variasi A/B)
- Catatan: Baca sinyal: ada hook/variasi yang jelas menang?

### 7. Scale & Optimization
_Output: Budget teralokasi ke pemenang · Estimasi: Sepanjang campaign · Tools: Ads Manager_
- R: Performance Marketer
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: —
- I: Stakeholder
- Catatan: Naikkan budget pemenang bertahap; matikan yang boros.

### 8. Reporting & Evaluation (Loop)
_Output: Report + next steps · Estimasi: 1–2 hari · Tools: Looker Studio, Sheet_
- R: Data & Insight Analyst
- A: Performance Marketer
- C: Social Media Lead
- I: Stakeholder
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Campaign Strategy & Brief
- Catatan: Learning → brief campaign berikutnya.
