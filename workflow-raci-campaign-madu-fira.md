## Detail per Tahap

### 1. Campaign Brief
_Output: Campaign brief · Estimasi: 2–4 jam · Tools: Notion, Docs_
- R: Social Media Strategist, Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Performance Marketer
- I: Stakeholder
- Catatan: Objective, audience, budget, timeline, success metric.

### 2. Riset
_Output: Landscape & insight campaign · Estimasi: 3–5 hari · Tools: Spreadsheet, Creative Center_
- R: Social Media Researcher
- A: Social Media Strategist
- C: Data & Insight Analyst
- I: —

### 3. Campaign Strategy & Plan
_Output: Strategi + rencana campaign · Estimasi: 3–5 hari · Tools: Notion, Sheet_
- R: Social Media Strategist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Creative Director, Performance Marketer
- I: Tim

### 4. Approval Plan (Approval)
_Output: Plan disetujui · Estimasi: 2–3 hari kerja · Tools: Email, Meet_
- R: Social Media Lead
- A: Marketing Manager, Klien
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: Tim
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Campaign Strategy & Plan
- Catatan: Ditolak → balik ke Strategy & Plan.

### 5. Creative Production
_Output: Aset campaign siap · Estimasi: 5–10 hari · Tools: Adobe Suite, CapCut, Figma_
- R: Creative Director, Graphic Designer, Video Editor, Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Social Media Strategist
- I: —

### 6. Review & Approval (Approval)
_Output: Aset final disetujui · Estimasi: 2–4 hari · Tools: Notion_
- R: Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead, Klien
- C: Creative Director
- I: —
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Creative Production
- Catatan: Ditolak → balik ke Production.

### 7. Launch + Monitoring
_Output: Campaign live + monitoring · Estimasi: Sepanjang campaign · Tools: Ads Manager, Analytics_
- R: Social Media Specialist, Performance Marketer
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Data & Insight Analyst
- I: Stakeholder

### 8. Post-Campaign Evaluation (Loop)
_Output: Report + rekomendasi · Estimasi: 3–5 hari · Tools: Looker Studio, Sheet_
- R: Data & Insight Analyst
- A: Social Media Strategist
- C: Social Media Lead
- I: Tim
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Campaign Brief
- Catatan: Learning → campaign berikutnya.
