# Workflow & RACI: Video Madu Fira

_6 tahap · disusun via Nakama SOP Playbook — Workflow & RACI Studio_

## Alur Tahapan

1. **Hook & Angle** →
2. **Script / Storyboard** →
3. **Shoot / Rekaman** →
4. **Edit & Subtitle** →
5. **Review Lead** [Approval] →
   ↩ balik ke: Edit & Subtitle
6. **Publish + Hook Test**

## Detail per Tahap

### 1. Hook & Angle
_Output: Hook + angle terpilih · Estimasi: 1–2 jam · Tools: Notion, TikTok Creative Center_
- R: Content Creator, Social Media Strategist
- A: Creative Director
- C: Copywriter
- I: —
- Catatan: Kunci di 0–3 detik. Tentukan sudut cerita sebelum apa pun ditulis.

### 2. Script / Storyboard
_Output: Script + shotlist · Estimasi: 2–4 jam · Tools: Google Docs_
- R: Copywriter
- A: Creative Director
- C: Videografer / Video Specialist
- I: —
- Catatan: Struktur hook–isi–CTA + shotlist singkat.

### 3. Shoot / Rekaman
_Output: Raw footage · Estimasi: 2–4 jam · Tools: Kamera/HP, Lighting_
- R: Videografer / Video Specialist, Content Creator
- A: Creative Director
- C: —
- I: —
- Catatan: Rekam sesuai shotlist; ambil beberapa take hook.

### 4. Edit & Subtitle
_Output: Video final + subtitle · Estimasi: 3–6 jam · Tools: CapCut, Premiere_
- R: Video Editor
- A: Creative Director
- C: Copywriter
- I: —
- Catatan: Subtitle wajib, ritme cepat, sound on-trend.

### 5. Review Lead (Approval)
_Output: Video disetujui · Estimasi: 30–60 menit · Tools: WhatsApp, Notion_
- R: Social Media Lead
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Creative Director
- I: —
- ↩ Kalau ditolak/berulang, balik ke: Edit & Subtitle
- Catatan: Ditolak → balik ke Edit (maks 1–2×).

### 6. Publish + Hook Test
_Output: Tayang + metrik hook · Estimasi: 15–30 menit · Tools: TikTok/IG Scheduler_
- R: Social Media Specialist
- A: Social Media Lead
- C: Data & Insight Analyst
- I: Tim
- Catatan: Catat retensi 3 detik & hook rate untuk bahan iterasi.
